# 📊 Customer Data Analysis Using Power BI

## Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior across 10 shopping malls in Istanbul (2021–2023) using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into customer demographics, purchasing patterns, revenue generation, product categories, and payment preferences.

---

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- CSV Dataset
- Data Visualization

---

## Business Questions

- Shopping distribution by gender
- Products sold by gender
- Revenue by gender
- Category distribution by gender
- Shopping distribution by age
- Products sold by age group
- Revenue by age group
- Category distribution by age group
- Payment method relationships
- Payment method distribution
- Business insights and recommendations

## Key Insights

- Female customers generated higher revenue than male customers.
- Female customers purchased the highest quantity of products.
- Customers aged 41–50 contributed the most purchases.
- Clothing is the top revenue-generating category.
- Shoes and Technology are strong revenue contributors.
- Cash is the most preferred payment method.

---

## Recommendations

- Focus marketing on female customers.
- Increase stock for Clothing, Shoes, and Technology.
- Promote digital payments.
- Introduce loyalty programs.
- Run age-based marketing campaigns.
